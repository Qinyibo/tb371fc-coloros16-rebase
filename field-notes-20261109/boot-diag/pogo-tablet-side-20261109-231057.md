## Concrete tablet-side root-cause hypothesis
1. **Primary (detect/power):** Tablet SMB5 never asserts POGO `present` (no DCIN voltage seen). Not a missing Magisk/tinno registration issue.
2. **Primary (MCU usable link):** Forcing i2c_hid resume proves tablet **can** talk briefly, but keyboard MCU reports **`kb_batt_level:0`** and **KB:0**, then disconnects — same failure mode as the historical 4s flicker. This is the tablet’s own telemetry of the pack.
3. **Secondary:** Hall driver missing → lid UX broken; not the blocker for HID probe.
4. **Dead end without GPIO:** Userspace cannot safely synthesize sustained MCU power; GPIO hold is known bootloop. Further Magisk/VINTF churn will not fix `present` or batt=0.
